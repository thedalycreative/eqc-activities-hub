About SKYWINGS

Our Story

SKYWINGS was founded in 2010 with a single goal: to make luxury resort travel effortless. What started as a boutique travel consultancy in Sydney has grown into a globally recognised resort booking platform with partners across 40+ destinations.

We believe that extraordinary travel shouldn't be complicated. Our curated selection of resorts, personalised service, and exclusive negotiated rates mean you spend less time planning and more time enjoying the trip of a lifetime.
