Singapore

The Lion City

Singapore punches above its weight as one of Asia's most extraordinary destinations. A gleaming metropolis where futuristic architecture, world-class dining, and tropical greenery meet in perfect harmony.

Often used as a stopover, Singapore deserves so much more of your time. Our resort partners on Sentosa Island and in the heart of the Marina Bay district offer extraordinary five-star experiences.

Must-Do Experiences

An extraordinary nature park spanning 101 hectares in the heart of Singapore. The iconic Supertree structures light up at night, and the two climate-controlled conservatories — the Flower Dome and the Cloud Forest — house plants from across the globe. A must-visit at dusk for the Garden Rhapsody light and sound show.

Perched atop three hotel towers, the Marina Bay Sands rooftop infinity pool is one of the most photographed views in the world. The SkyPark offers panoramic views over the city skyline, Marina Bay, and beyond. Only hotel guests can access the pool — another reason to book through SKYWINGS.

Singapore's hawker centres are UNESCO-recognised cultural heritage. Maxwell Food Centre and Lau Pa Sat offer thousands of dishes from Chinese, Malay, Indian, and Peranakan cuisines — all at incredible value. From Michelin-starred Hainanese chicken rice to freshly made roti prata, this is a food lover's paradise.

Located on Sentosa Island, Universal Studios Singapore is Southeast Asia's only Universal theme park. With 7 themed zones including Jurassic World and Transformers, it's an excellent day out for families and thrill-seekers alike. Our Sentosa Island resort packages include park entry.

Orchard Road is a 2.2km retail strip lined with over 20 world-class malls. From international luxury brands at ION Orchard to local boutiques at Mandarin Gallery, shopping in Singapore is a destination experience in itself. Many of our partner hotels are within walking distance.
