Australia

Discover Australia's Finest Resorts

From the coral reefs of the Whitsundays to the golden sands of the Gold Coast — Australia offers world-class resort experiences unlike anywhere else on earth.

Gold Coast

Gold Coast, Queensland

The Gold Coast is Australia's playground — 57 kilometres of golden beaches, world-famous surf, theme parks, and a buzzing nightlife. Our handpicked resort partners offer direct beach access and exclusive packages for every traveller.

  • Beachfront dining and rooftop bars
  • World Surf League competition beaches
  • Theme parks: Movie World, Sea World, Wet'n'Wild
  • Lamington National Park hinterland escapes
Book a Gold Coast Package
Whitsundays

The Whitsundays, Queensland

Nestled in the heart of the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park, the Whitsundays is home to 74 tropical islands. Hayman Island and Hamilton Island offer world-class all-inclusive resorts surrounded by some of the planet's most pristine ocean.

  • Great Barrier Reef snorkelling and diving
  • Whitehaven Beach — rated one of the world's best
  • Sailing charters and seaplane tours
  • Award-winning resort dining
