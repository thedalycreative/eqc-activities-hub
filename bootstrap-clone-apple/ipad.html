iPad Specifications

Uses the Accordion component. Docs: Accordion

13-inch or 11-inch Ultra Retina XDR display. Featuring ProMotion technology with adaptive refresh rates up to 120Hz, P3 wide colour, and nano-texture display glass option.

Apple M4 chip. 9-core CPU with 3 performance and 6 efficiency cores. 10-core GPU. 16-core Neural Engine.

12MP Wide camera. Adaptive True Tone flash. 12MP Ultra Wide front camera with Centre Stage. 4K video recording, ProRes video, and Slow-mo support.

Up to 10 hours of web browsing on Wi-Fi. Built-in 36.7-watt-hour lithium-polymer battery. USB-C connector for charging.
