Frequently Asked Questions

Turn on your iPhone and follow the setup assistant. You can transfer data from your old device using Quick Start, restore from an iCloud backup, or set up as new. Make sure both devices are charged and connected to Wi-Fi.

AppleCare+ extends your warranty and adds accidental damage protection. It covers up to two incidents of accidental damage every 12 months, with a service fee. It's recommended for devices you use daily.

Go to Settings > General > About on your device, or visit checkcoverage.apple.com and enter your device serial number. You can find your serial number in Settings > General > About.

You have 14 days from the date of delivery to return most Apple products. Items must be in their original condition with packaging. Visit an Apple Store or start a return online through your order history.
